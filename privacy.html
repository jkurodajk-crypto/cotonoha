Privacy Policy

Cotonoha ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who visits our shop or reads our pages.

1. What we collect

Analytics. Pages viewed, time of visit, approximate region, device and browser type. This does not identify you personally.

Order information. When you buy through a platform such as Etsy or BOOTH, that platform passes us the details of your order.

Messages. Whatever you write to us.

We never receive your card number or bank details. Payment is handled entirely by the platform.

2. Why we use it

Only to deliver what you bought, to answer you, and to improve what we make.

3. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, except where the law requires it.

4. Services we use

Google Analytics (analytics on our Etsy and BOOTH shops), Etsy and BOOTH (selling), and Pinterest, Instagram, X, note and stand.fm (publishing).

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy. Google Analytics uses cookies; you can stop this with Google's opt-out add-on.

5. Access, correction, deletion

If you would like to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, write to us. We will confirm who you are and act promptly.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page.

7. Contact

cotonoha.houme.labo@gmail.com

You can also message us through our Etsy shop, CotonohaJP.
